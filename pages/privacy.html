How Kuha protects you

What we store

Your username, display name, bio, a hashed password and your posts. All of it sits in your browser's localStorage on this device.

What we never do

No cookies for tracking, no analytics, no ads, no network requests. Nothing is sent to anyone, so nothing can be sold or used to train AI.

Your control

On your profile you can hide like counts, download your data as JSON, or delete your account and posts.
