About Kuha

Kuha comes from the Bisaya word for "take". Take the shot, take the edit, take it home. It is a social space for photographers and videographers who want to share work without being watched.

Who it is for

Wedding shooters, same-day editors, student filmmakers, and anyone behind a lens.

What you can do

Post frames, tag them as photo, video, behind the scenes or tips, and like other creators' work.

Why it exists

A midterm prototype showing a social app can work with no backend and no data collection.
